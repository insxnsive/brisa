Discord goes out through a tunnel. The rest of the PC does not.

In Brazil, Discord Go Live and camera don't work. Brisa sends only Discord through a WireGuard tunnel to a server outside Brazil. Your browser, your games and the rest of the PC stay on the normal connection.

Download for Windows
  • Windows 11 x64
  • open source, GPL-3.0

How it works

  1. Connect closes Discord (leave any call first), brings up a WireGuard tunnel for it alone and opens Discord again.
  2. Disconnect closes Discord, drops the tunnel and opens Discord again on the normal route.
  3. Nothing else on the PC ever enters the tunnel. Not the browser, not your games.

What it needs

Windows
x64, built for Windows 11.
Administrator
Windows asks for administrator rights to connect and disconnect. Opening the app does not.
Account
Sign in with your Proton account (the tunnel ends at a Proton server) or import a WireGuard .conf under Settings, Advanced.
WireSock
Not bundled. On the first connection Brisa downloads the installer for one specific version, checks its SHA-256 and opens the vendor's installer.

Install

  1. Download and run Brisa-win-Setup.exe.
  2. Open Brisa from the Start menu, sign in with Proton or import your .conf, and click Connect.
